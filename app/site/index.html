How it works

Set up once.Stream forever.

1

Install in one click

The installer sets up everything, adds the Offleak filter to OBS, and picks the fastest hardware it finds: your NVIDIA GPU, Apple Silicon, or DirectML on AMD and Intel.

2

Choose who stays visible

Snap the people in front of your webcam or drop in a photo. Group photos are split into one card per person. Toggle faces, QR codes and phone numbers independently.

3

Go live, protected

Add Offleak filter to your camera in OBS. A short safety buffer lets Offleak see a moment ahead, and if it is ever unsure, it blurs the whole frame instead of guessing.

Is Offleak really free?
Yes. Offleak is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence. There is no account, subscription or upload. The installers include the full source code.
Does my video go to a server?
No. Detection, face matching and blurring all run on your own computer. After installation Offleak works offline. The optional Twitch connection only reads your live status and moderator alerts. It never sends video.
Which graphics card do I need?
On a Mac, any Apple Silicon chip. On Windows, an NVIDIA GPU gives the best speed through CUDA. AMD and Intel GPUs accelerate text reading through DirectML, and Offleak still runs on the CPU without a GPU.
Can it stream at 60 fps?
Yes. Choose any frame rate from 5 to 60 fps in Offleak's settings. Every output frame is blurred: the detectors run as often as your computer allows, and motion tracking carries each blur onto the frames in between. 60 fps needs a recent GPU or Apple Silicon. If your computer cannot keep up, Offleak drops frames instead of letting the delay grow, so pick a lower rate on older machines.
Why does my computer warn me when I open it?
Early releases are not yet signed with paid Apple and Microsoft certificates. On macOS, right-click the installer and choose Open. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway.
Will it catch everything?
No detector is perfect, and Offleak is still early software. It is built to fail safe: if it loses track, it blurs the whole frame rather than guess. Test with dummy information, check your output before going live, and keep a stream delay. Audio is not filtered.
